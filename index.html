GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions,
before we clean.

Do you serve my city or municipality?

We serve Metro Manila / NCR and CALABARZON / Region IV-A: Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, and Quezon Province. Quezon Province is different from Quezon City in NCR. Contact us to confirm service availability and scheduling in your area; not every service is available in every location.

How much will my cleaning cost?

Your quotation depends on the service, property size, condition, access, and requested extras. Send those details through the inquiry form; no price or appointment is confirmed by submission.

Is my appointment confirmed when I send the form?

No. Your inquiry starts the conversation. The team must agree with you on scope, price, and schedule before an appointment is confirmed.

Which services can I request?

You can inquire about residential, office, Airbnb turnover, commercial cleaning, and car wash. The team confirms the suitable scope and availability for your location before booking.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Provide your city or municipality and province, property type and floor area or vehicle type, current condition, priority tasks, access arrangements, and preferred dates. These help define the scope and scheduling; ask the team to confirm any location-dependent costs.

Can I request recurring cleaning?

Yes, you can request recurring residential or office cleaning. Share your preferred frequency and days. The team must confirm availability, scope, and the schedule for your location.
