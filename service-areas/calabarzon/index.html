Five provinces. Location-specific planning.

CALABARZON coverage includes Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, and Quezon Province. It does not mean every service is available at every address. Start with the city or municipality and province, requested work, and preferred dates. The team will confirm suitability, access, and scheduling before you book.

Regional coverage describes where customers can inquire; it does not imply a Sunshine branch, office, or local team in each province. For a property in NCR instead, see Metro Manila cleaning coverage.

CaviteLagunaBatangasRizalQuezon Province

Cavite

For a Cavite home, condo, workplace, or other premises, include the city or municipality rather than only the province. If the property is in a subdivision or managed development, mention entry registration, parking, and permitted work hours. For a move-in, say whether the property is vacant and whether renovation work is finished.

Laguna

For Laguna inquiries, describe how the property is used and which rooms or shared spaces need attention. A recurring home or office clean needs a preferred frequency; a one-off deep clean needs a description of the current condition. If the property is a business premises, explain its use so the team can confirm whether the requested tasks fit its capabilities. Industrial or specialist cleaning is not assumed.

Batangas

For a Batangas property, give your municipality or city and available access dates. If requesting Airbnb turnover, provide departure and next-arrival times so feasibility can be checked. For an occasionally occupied property, describe its current condition and who will provide access. Laundry, linens, and restocking require separate confirmation.

Rizal

For a Rizal inquiry, include the exact city or municipality and any access restrictions relevant to bringing in cleaning tools. Mention stairs, parking arrangements, or areas that cannot be reached safely where these apply. If the work follows renovation, identify dust, residue, and surface materials so the team can assess the scope rather than assume a standard treatment.

Quezon Province

Please write “Quezon Province” together with your city or municipality. This is different from Quezon City, which belongs to Metro Manila / NCR. Give a preferred date range, property type, and requested tasks so availability and scheduling can be assessed for the correct location. Confirm any location-related costs in the quotation before booking.

Do you serve every municipality in CALABARZON?

We accept inquiries across Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, and Quezon Province. Service availability and scheduling are confirmed for each location and requested task; region-wide coverage is not a guarantee for every municipality.

Is car wash available in all five provinces?

Do not assume this. Email hello@sunshinecleaningservices.ph with the vehicle type, city or municipality, and province. The team must confirm the available wash scope, service location, and schedule.

Are prices and travel arrangements the same as NCR?

No identical regional pricing or free travel is promised. Ask for a complete quotation for your specific property and location, including any applicable location-related requirements or costs.

Can I request recurring cleaning outside Metro Manila?

Yes, you may request recurring residential or office cleaning. Provide your municipality, province, preferred frequency, and days. Availability and the actual schedule need confirmation.

How do I enter my province in the form?

Include your city or municipality and province in the quotation form. For Quezon Province, spell out the province to distinguish it from Quezon City. Share relevant access requirements and preferred dates where the form allows.

Does sending the form confirm a visit?

No. The team must agree with you on suitability, scope, full price, access, and dates before the appointment is confirmed.
