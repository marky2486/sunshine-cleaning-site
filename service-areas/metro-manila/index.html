METRO MANILA / NCR

Cleaning services
in Metro Manila.

From a condo reset to a recurring office clean, tell Sunshine about your space in NCR. Confirm your location, requested service, and scheduling before booking.

Start with your NCR location.

We welcome Metro Manila inquiries, including Makati, BGC / Taguig, and Quezon City. Give the complete city and property type. Availability is checked for your requested service and dates; coverage is not a promise of same-day cleaning or a slot at every address.

For a property outside NCR in Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, or Quezon Province, use our CALABARZON cleaning coverage guide.

Plan the building access with the clean.

If your condo, office, or managed building has visitor registration, contractor permits, loading rules, lift reservations, or limited parking, include those requirements. Tell us the unit or floor information needed for planning without posting your address publicly.

For an occupied office, explain whether work continues during cleaning and which areas are restricted. For a guest stay, provide checkout and next check-in times. These details help the team assess the scope and a realistic cleaning window.

Choose the work your property needs.

Residential inquiries can cover regular cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in or move-out, and post-construction work. Office cleaning focuses on workplace spaces; commercial inquiries concern other premises and need a capability check. Airbnb turnover and car wash also need an agreed scope and confirmed availability.

  • Residential cleaning

  • Office cleaning

    Thoughtful care for workstations, meeting rooms, pantries, and workplace washrooms.

  • Airbnb turnover cleaning

    Turnover cleaning coordinated around your property, checkout, and next arrival.

  • Commercial cleaning

  • Car wash

A clear scope, a documented finish.

Sunshine’s Maarteng Cleaner approach uses checklists, color-coded tools, and job documentation. Agree on the tasks and records for your visit in the quotation, then review the completed work with the team.

NCR INQUIRIES

Before your Metro Manila clean.

Do you serve my city in NCR?

Send your Metro Manila city, property type, service, and preferred dates. The team checks location-specific availability and scheduling before confirming a booking.

Is Quezon City part of this region?

Yes. Quezon City is in Metro Manila / NCR. Quezon Province is a different location in CALABARZON; select the CALABARZON guide if that is where your property is.

What if my building requires permits?

Tell us the building requirements and any advance registration or access windows when you inquire. Responsibilities and arrangements must be confirmed before the visit.

Can I arrange a recurring home or office clean?

You can request a recurring schedule. Provide preferred frequency, days, property size, and priority tasks; the team confirms what it can accommodate.

When is my cleaning schedule confirmed?

After the team and customer agree on service scope, price, date, and access arrangements. Submitting the inquiry form alone does not reserve a slot.
