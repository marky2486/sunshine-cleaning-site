Inquiries and appointments

Submitting the website form is an inquiry. It does not confirm an appointment, a price, or acceptance of a particular cleaning scope. Confirm these details directly with the team before making arrangements.

Scope and quotation

Service pages help you describe what you need. The written quotation should specify the agreed tasks, exclusions, extras, location, access, and scheduling. Car-wash scope and coverage require confirmation.

Preparing for the visit

Discuss building access, parking, permits, surface sensitivities, valuables, pets, and any restricted or hazardous areas before the appointment. Ask what preparation is needed for your specific service.

Changes, payment, and concerns

Ask the team for its current payment, cancellation, rescheduling, refund, and concerns-handling terms before confirming your booking. These pages do not set fees, guarantees, refund commitments, or liability limits.
