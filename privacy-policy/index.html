Practical information about this static website and its externally hosted inquiry form.

The website and inquiry form

The main pages are static HTML. This website does not run its own inquiry database or backend. The quotation form is embedded using Deftform. Information submitted through it is processed by this external form provider; the website does not store submissions in a local database.

Information you choose to share

The Deftform quotation form collects the information you enter into its fields. Share only information relevant to your cleaning request. Avoid including sensitive personal information in free-text notes.

Third-party connections

Opening a page with the form connects your browser to Deftform and its required services. The embed passes the page URL and referring-page information to Deftform. The provider may handle technical information and cookies under its own practices. Ask for the current privacy notice if you need details before submitting information.

Questions about your information

Use the contact inquiry page to ask about information you have shared, including requests concerning access or correction. Do not send additional sensitive information merely to identify an inquiry.

Business privacy notice

Ask the team for its current privacy notice before providing information if you need details about retention, access, sharing, or request handling.
